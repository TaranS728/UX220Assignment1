# Career & Creativity

![Career and Creativity](images/career.jpg)

Since graduating from the UX Design program, I have been able to start a career in UX and build on the skills that I learned in school. 

I have had a chance to work on real projects where I have been able to use my creativity and problem solving skills to design better experiences for users.

Along with that, I have also built a strong portfolio with projects that showcase my skill set, and I have become much more confident with my skills in user research, prototyping, usability testing, and web designing. I have also continued learning new things after graduating and have taken on projects that have challenged me and helped me grow as a designer.
