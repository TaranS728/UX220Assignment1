# Research - Compare and Contrast

In the article “Goal Setting and Student Achievement: A Longitudinal Study”, it is discussed that effective goals should be specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time bound, and my goals for what I want to have accomplished within the five years after graduating all fall into these categories. All my goals are specific, they lay out a timeline for when I want them completed by, they are all attainable and relevant to what I consider as “success”, and all within a reasonable window. 

Both my personal goals and the ideas discussed in the article focus on personal growth, learning, and working towards meaningful accomplishments, which makes them very similar. One difference is that the article focuses on academic achievements, where as my goals shift focus to other areas in my life; health, relationships, and experiences outside of school. 

## Reference

Moeller, A. J., Theiler, J. M., & Wu, C. (2012). Goal setting and student achievement: A longitudinal study. *The Modern Language Journal, 96*(2), 153–169.
